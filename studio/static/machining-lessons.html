Section a loft


A duct rises 20 mm from a 40 × 24 mm rectangular base to a circular outlet. Change the outlet and slide the cutting plane through the duct. Can you make a 31 × 23 mm section halfway up, with its centre 2 mm to the right of the base?













How to predict the section
At 10 mm the plane is halfway up the duct. Average the base width and outlet diameter to get the section width. Average the base depth and outlet diameter to get its depth. The section centre travels the same fraction of the outlet offset.


Try it in Studio. In 3D CAD, add a Two-section loft. Set the base to Rectangle, 40 × 24 mm, and the top to Circle. Copy the outlet diameter and X offset above, leave the Y offset at zero and set the loft height to 20 mm. With the origin at Z = 0 and no rotation, choose Horizontal section, enter the same Z inside the solid and choose Prepare in CAM. Select the contour to check its width and height in Properties.
